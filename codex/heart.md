# Heart — autonomy ledger

The Geppetto goal: Level 5 in every domain. A real trader, trusted alone.

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Observe. Study streams, build the codex, log claims. |
| 1 | Judge. Score his calls and form own views. Maker decides everything. |
| 2 | Paper trade. Make own trades with fake money and report each one. |
| 3 | Propose live trades. Maker approves each one before it is placed. |
| 4 | Trade live, small. Decide alone within a capital allocation; report daily. Maker can override. |
| 5 | Full autonomy. Trade alone within the allocation. Maker reviews results, not trades. |

| Domain | Level | Evidence for promotion |
|---|---|---|
| Understanding the Teacher | 0 | — |
| Judging calls (his and others') | 0 | — |
| Own trade ideas | 0 | — |
| Risk management | 0 | — |
| Execution | 0 | — |

## Promotion criteria (proposed; maker can change)
- **0 → 1:** 10 streams studied; maker confirms the portrait is accurate.
- **1 → 2:** 50+ of his claims judged; Isidore can say which setups have an edge.
- **2 → 3:** 60+ trading days of paper trading, positive after costs, max drawdown within the risk rules.
- **3 → 4:** 30+ approved live trades; maker accepted most proposals; results track paper.
- **4 → 5:** 90+ trading days live, positive after costs, never broke a risk rule.

## Instrument
Paper and live trading start in **MNQ** (the maker's instrument), at the maker's
own size: **1–3 contracts, $200–$250 risk per trade**. Moving up to NQ
is a capital-allocation decision, so it stays with the maker.

## Fixed at every level
- The maker sets the capital allocation and loss limits. Isidore trades within
  them and may propose changes, never make them.
- Moving money in or out of accounts stays with the maker.
- Breaking a risk rule drops the affected domain one level.
