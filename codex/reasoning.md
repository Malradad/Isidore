# Reasoning — how he reads the market

## Top-down bias (~)
- Starts on higher timeframes (1M, 1W, 1D, 4H, 1H) to decide direction.
- Bias tools: HTF inversions, equal highs/lows, old highs/lows sitting inside FVGs.
- Only takes iFVGs that agree with that bias.

## Liquidity (~)
- Price is drawn to liquidity: places where stops cluster (equal highs/lows,
  old highs/lows, data-release highs/lows).
- "Internal liquidity" = those levels inside the current range. If it hasn't
  been taken yet, it is the target.
- He has a specific "internal liquidity rule" (YouTube playlist). Not yet known in detail.

## Time (~)
- Time of day is a filter, not a detail. Setups only count inside killzones.

## Why liquidity works, in his words (~, 2026)
- Big players need other people's orders to fill. They fill where orders cluster:
  stop losses and breakout entries beyond obvious old highs and lows.
- They don't "hunt" retail on purpose. The reaction just happens where the orders are.
- So an obvious level + a sharp V-shaped reaction = proof a big player filled there.
  The iFVG after it puts him "on the right side of smart money."
- Tiny sweeps inside a range = not enough orders = not real liquidity.
