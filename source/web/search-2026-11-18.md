## Strategy (as reported)
- Core: iFVG (inversion fair value gap), layered on ICT concepts.
- His definition (X): a bearish FVG that is CLOSED above becomes support;
  a bullish FVG that is CLOSED below becomes resistance.
- His entry (X thread): enters on the candle close through the FVG, does not wait for a retrace.
- Bias from higher timeframes: 1H, 4H, 1D, 1W, 1M (course page, secondhand).
  Bias tools listed: HTF inversions, equal highs/lows, old highs/lows inside FVGs.
- Killzones: London, New York AM, New York PM. iFVGs outside a killzone
  "typically ignored" (unofficial promo site).
- Targets: internal liquidity — data highs/lows, old highs/lows inside FVGs, equal highs/lows.
  "Goes long when internal liquidity has not yet been hit"; has a YouTube playlist on his internal liquidity rule.
- Premium "iFVG Rating Guide" grades setups on: timeframe confluence, killzone
  alignment, HTF bias agreement, gap quality, liquidity context.
- Markets: NQ (main), ES, gold, forex majors.
- Indicators on TradingView: "iFVG Ultimate+", "DD IFVG [Pro+]" (invite-only; officialness unclear).
