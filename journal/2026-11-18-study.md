# Study — raw Izzy iFVG v0.4 paper forward test (2026-11-18)

*Bot.* Requested by Izzy; **approved by the maker in chat** (05:22 NY), including suspending the daily 2-loss / −$500 limit for this study.

## Rules
- Paper Trading "Isidore" only. Take **every CONFIRMED** alert from 30s/1m/2m/3m/5m, with no judgment filter.
- **1 MNQ per trade.** Max risk ≤ $250. One position at a time; a CONFIRMED that arrives while in a position is logged "skipped: in position".
- Every position has a stop (pre-staged ticket with an 80-tick safety bracket, then moved to the alert's exact stop/target). Never the hotkeys or the floating chart buttons.
- Stop and flatten if: a real broker appears, the symbol is not MNQ, an error/unknown dialog, or the balance is < $47,500.

## Execution mechanism
An in-page trigger (MutationObserver in the TradingView tab) watches the alert toast/log for `"izzy":"CONFIRMED"`. Before clicking it checks: fresh alert (bar_close < 60 s old), side, symbol MNQ1!, risk_pts × $2 ≤ $250, armed, Paper Trading + "Isidore" present, balance ≥ $47,500, no open position, and a valid ticket (1 unit, both bracket switches ON, button text "Buy/Sell 1 MNQZ2026 MARKET"). Then it sets the ticket side and clicks submit once, and disarms. Duplicates (same tf + side + bar_close) are ignored. Bot wakes via the webhook waiter, fixes the SL/TP to the alert prices, re-stages and re-arms.
