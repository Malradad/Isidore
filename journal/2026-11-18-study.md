## Rules
- Paper Trading "Isidore" only. Take **every CONFIRMED** alert from 30s/1m/2m/3m/5m, with no judgment filter.
- **1 MNQ per trade.** Max risk ≤ $250. One position at a time; a CONFIRMED that arrives while in a position is logged "skipped: in position".
- Every position has a stop (pre-staged ticket with an 80-tick safety bracket, then moved to the alert's exact stop/target). Never the hotkeys or the floating chart buttons.
- Stop and flatten if: a real broker appears, the symbol is not MNQ, an error/unknown dialog, or the balance is < $47,500.

## Execution mechanism
An in-page trigger (MutationObserver in the TradingView tab) watches the alert toast/log for `"izzy":"CONFIRMED"`. Before clicking it checks: fresh alert (bar_close < 60 s old), side, symbol MNQ1!, risk_pts × $2 ≤ $250, armed, Paper Trading + "Isidore" present, balance ≥ $47,500, no open position, and a valid ticket (1 unit, both bracket switches ON, button text "Buy/Sell 1 MNQZ2026 MARKET"). Then it sets the ticket side and clicks submit once, and disarms. Duplicates (same tf + side + bar_close) are ignored. Bot wakes via the webhook waiter, fixes the SL/TP to the alert prices, re-stages and re-arms.

## Rule: one trade at a time, let it pan out (maker via Izzy, 05:49 NY) — Bot
1. While a position is open: **no new entries, no adding, no reversing**, from any timeframe. Every CONFIRMED during the trade is logged "skipped: in position" with its JSON.
2. A trade ends only by its planned exit: the stop, the target, the close-back-through-gap rule (when that's its stop mode), or the **16:55 NY EOD flat**. No early manual exits, except the safety stops (real broker, wrong symbol, error/unknown dialog, balance < $47,500).
3. **Enforced in the trigger code (v2, 05:49):** the trigger disarms the moment it clicks. Before every click it checks the open position *twice*: at detection, and again right before submitting (the Positions tab count or any position-row ✕ button means open). An `inPos` flag set at the click covers the gap until the position row appears. It re-arms only after the position is confirmed flat, the ticket is re-staged (1 unit, both bracket switches ON), and ≥ 20 s have passed since the last click.
4. **No late entries:** after an exit, any alert whose bar_close is at or before the exit time is refused ("bar closed during previous trade"); only the next fresh CONFIRMED is taken. The EOD flat at 16:55 NY is also handled in the page.
