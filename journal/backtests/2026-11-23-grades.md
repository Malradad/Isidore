## P7: "no shorts with equal highs / data high above, no longs with equal lows / data low below"
Coding (~):
- **Equal highs/lows:** two untaken swing points within 8 ticks above (short) / below (long) the entry.
- **Data high/low:** the 08:30 candle's high/low on days when that candle's range is ≥ 1.5× the 20-bar average (a
  news-spike proxy; there is no calendar in Pine). The level counts until price trades through it.

| 5m, all grades | Without | With P7 | Trades kept |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2019–22 | PF 0.86 (5.7/wk) | **0.93** (1.5/wk) | 26% |
| 2023–26 | PF 0.92 (5.9/wk) | **0.89** (1.6/wk) | 26% |

**Read ~:** it helps in one half and hurts in the other, so it does **not** hold. As coded it is far too broad: some
untaken equal pair sits above almost every short, so it removes three trades in four. His rule is probably about
*obvious, nearby* equal highs and a real data wick. A tighter version (equal pair within ~1R of the entry, real
CPI/NFP dates) is the next test. Not judged on its own spirit, only on this coding.
