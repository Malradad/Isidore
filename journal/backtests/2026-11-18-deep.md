# Deep backtest: Izzy iFVG v0.4 + claim tests, 2026-11-18

First test on real sample size. Run through the official TradingView MCP connector
(Deep Backtesting, server-side) on **CME_MINI:MNQ1!, 1-minute, 2025-01-01 → 2026-11-17**
(~23 months, all sessions). No orders were placed; this is simulation only.

Costs as in v0.4: $0.62/contract/side, 1 tick slippage on market and stop orders. Size: $225 risk, max 3 MNQ.
Daily limits (v0.4 backtest defaults): max 3 trades, stop after 2 losses or −$500.

## Engines and how they were checked ✓
- **Python port** `tools/backtest/izzy_bt.py` (bar-by-bar copy of `tools/pine/isidore_ifvg.pine` v0.4).
  On the connector's bars it reproduces TradingView's Strategy Tester **trade for trade, to the cent**:
  5m 49/49 trades after the first day (the first day differs only because TradingView's history starts
  1½ h earlier), 1m 15/15. One fix was needed to get there: Pine's `ta.pivothigh/low` allows ties on
  the left, not the right.
- **Lab script** `tools/pine/isidore_ifvg_lab.pine`, saved in the maker's TradingView as
  **"Izzy iFVG lab"** (`USER;8bb6ebadfe81498ca4bc46c244b1db00`). Same rules as v0.4, no alerts, plus
  switchable v0.5 filters. Checked against Python with four filters on (5m): same 4 trades, same −$212.44.
- The live script "Izzy iFVG" (v0.4, alerts) was **not touched**.

## Izzy's read ~
- **v0.4 has no edge.** 1,264 trades over 23 months, PF 0.91, −$5.9K after costs. The early small-sample
  wins (Nov 18 Strategy Tester, 10–18 trades) were noise, as feared. Paper trading v0.4 signals as-is
  is practice for execution, not a strategy.
- **One trade a day is the only change that turns it positive** (PF 1.05, +$1.1K, drawdown cut 4×).
  That fits his own rule. But PF 1.05 is thin: within noise of break-even and easily erased by worse
  fills (Test 4 lost 6 pts to the chase alone). Promising, not proven.
- **First sweep vs 3rd+ sweep: no difference** (0.91 vs 0.93). Claim 4 not supported as coded.
- **Engineered targets do a bit better than single highs** (0.93 vs 0.88), but both lose. Direction
  supports claim 5; no edge.
- **Momentum candles: no difference** (0.90 vs 0.90). Claim 10 not supported with body ≥ 60% of range.
- **09:30–10:00 alone: still losing** (0.92, 166 trades). Claim 9 not supported on its own.
- **My fuel target hurts**: aiming for the top of the chain drops the win rate to 14%. Either my
  definition of fuel is wrong or far targets don't pay on 1m. Needs his chart examples, not tuning.
- **His full A+ stack fires 13 times in 23 months.** Mechanically, the A+ setup is too rare to test;
  what he really trades must include judgement my filters don't capture.
- One fuel-run loss of −$1,648 (risk was $225) shows gap risk when a trade is held across a short/holiday
  session. The EOD flat at 16:55 does not cover early closes.

## Caveats

### Izzy's read ~
- **Killzones make v0.4 worse, not better** (PF 0.85 vs 0.91 all hours). Restricting to his hours does not
  rescue the mechanical model.

  delivering from and to), not a moving average. That is untested and needs his chart examples to code.

  conclusion: the iFVG trigger alone has no edge on MNQ 1m; any edge must be in his discretionary context.
